## Account skills copied here

`skills/awwwards-3d`, `skills/3d-web-build-playbook`, `skills/performance`, `skills/systematic-debugging` are the Freebuff account skills used on these builds. The versions here are compact working copies; the originals stay in the account so they are available in every chat.

`performance` is authored by web-quality-skills (MIT, v2.0). `systematic-debugging` is from the superpowers skill family. Keep their attribution if you redistribute.
